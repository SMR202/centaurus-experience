# Centaurus experience

An independent, guest-first client demonstration for The Centaurus Mall, Islamabad. Built from an empty workspace; no existing app or indoor navigation engine was present. Deliver a React Native/Expo app with five tabs: Home, Explore, Offers, Club, You.

## Scope
Seven complete local journeys: Adidas search and route; drop claim and wallet; sample receipt and reward; saved parking; evening itinerary; step-free routing; retailer submission and administrator approval visible in Offers.

## Truthfulness
Merchant names are checked against the official directory. Unknown real floors remain unconfirmed. Synthetic graph floors are explicitly separate from merchant floor facts. All offers, membership, receipts, films, events, timing and operations figures are demonstrations. No backend, sign-in, payments, real positioning, tracking, OCR, booking or redemption.

## Delivery criteria
TypeScript strict; persistent local state; bundled assets and fonts; safe areas; accessible controls; deterministic simulation; functional reset; test evidence and genuine screenshots. Native device limitations must be documented, never represented as tested.
