# Asset licenses

Original licensed assets are recorded in `assets/provenance.json`. The 10 October iteration adds public official-brand logos, storefront photographs and campaign references requested by the user for a private pitch. These are recorded individually in `assets/brand-provenance.json`, with source page, original image URL, local file and copyright status. Public availability is not an open license; no production publication permission has been established. Brand owners retain all rights. Obtain clearance before external/public distribution. No discount or retailer endorsement is inferred from this creative.

There are 26 source-backed merchant logo entries, 22 storefront images and 3 researched campaign images (two campaign images are used in the UI; SUKOON is retained as research). The Outfitters image is its official mobile campaign banner. Logos are contained without redrawing; photos are resized/converted to WebP, with layout crops. Source pages were checked on 10 October 2026.

Sources: [Centaurus directory](https://thecentaurusmall.com/brands/), [SAPPHIRE](https://pk.sapphireonline.pk/), [Outfitters](https://outfitters.com.pk/). The original photo/font/icon licenses below remain unchanged.

Reference screenshots are for internal design research, separate from app assets, and remain copyrighted by their respective owners. Mobbin access was unavailable; no gated images were extracted.
