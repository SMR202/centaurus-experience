# Data provenance

Checked 9 October 2026. Primary sources: https://thecentaurusmall.com/brands/ and https://thecentaurusmall.com/team_group/shop/ . The brand index confirms names, not live tenancy guarantees. Merchant-level source URLs are retained in data.

The shop index explicitly lists Cinnabon on Ground Floor, KOA on Third, Taana Baana on First, Imrozia Serene on Second and Bachaa Party on Third. These are sourced directory facts, subject to change. Several descriptions on the index appear mismatched (e.g. Nine Figures text referring to Cross Stitch); these descriptions are not reused. Failed or unavailable detail pages are not a basis for floor claims.

Other included names are present in the brand index. Their floor labels read “Floor confirmation pending”. Demo nodes are illustrative placements, not evidence of real floors. Service points, parking geometry, all routing measurements, campaigns, films, events and membership records are synthetic and carry demo metadata.
